Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026. Last Updated: October 31, 2026.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Nexora Tools (accessible at nexoraworldonline.online). We recognize the profound importance of digital privacy and transparency. This Privacy Policy details our operational architecture, the absolute separation between our website and your uploaded files, how browser cookies and local storage are utilized, and our guidelines regarding third-party advertising networks.

2. Our Core Architecture: 100% Client-Side Processing

The defining technical design of Nexora Tools is that all tool operations execute entirely within your local web browser client. When you use tools such as the Image Compressor, Image Resizer, Image Format Converter, JPG to PDF, PDF Merge, JSON Formatter, Password Generator, or Financial/Health Calculators:

  • No File Uploads: Files selected through file pickers or drag-and-drop zones are read directly into browser RAM via the HTML5 FileReader API and WebAssembly / Canvas primitives. They are never transmitted across the network to any server.
  • No Database Storage: We do not operate database clusters or cloud object stores to retain your personal documents, photos, text, or formulas.
  • Zero Session Persistence: As soon as you navigate away from or close the browser tab, all file buffers and calculated datasets are instantly and permanently purged from volatile computer memory.

3. Information We Collect Automatically

Because Nexora Tools is delivered as a static website hosted across Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) and GitHub Pages, standard web server access logs may passively collect routine technical network data. This includes:

  • Your Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type, language, and operating system.
  • Referring URL and requested page paths.
  • Date and time stamps of page requests.

This technical log data is collected strictly for security diagnostics, DDoS mitigation, and aggregate performance optimization. It is never linked to personally identifiable information.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Nexora Tools does not use invasive first-party tracking cookies. We utilize modern browser localStorage strictly for user experience personalization:

  • nexora-theme: Stores your preference for "dark" or "light" mode so your chosen appearance persists upon your next visit.

You can clear or disable Local Storage at any time within your browser settings without impacting the core functionality of any tool.

5. Third-Party Advertising & Ad Vendors

To sustain free access to our tools, Nexora Tools displays non-intrusive advertisements served by trusted third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense). These third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, and JavaScript to serve personalized advertisements based on your prior visits to this and other websites across the Internet:

  • Google and DART Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opting Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info or adjusting Google Ad Settings at Google Ad Settings.

6. Third-Party CDNs & External Resources

To deliver optimized font typography and lightweight client-side scripts, our static pages load resources from reputable Content Delivery Networks (such as Google Fonts, cdnjs, or unpkg for pdf-lib and qrcodejs). These providers may log IP addresses and standard browser headers in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

7. GDPR and CCPA Compliance

For visitors residing within the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or California under the CCPA/CPRA:

  • Because Nexora Tools does not require account registration, collect user names, or store files on servers, we do not maintain consumer profiles or sell personal data.
  • You retain the right to manage cookies and third-party advertising consent via browser settings and standard ad-choice mechanisms.

8. Children's Information

Nexora Tools does not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Our tools are educational, mathematical, and productivity utilities available to general audiences.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological advancements or regulatory revisions. Any updates will be published immediately on this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
